Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who maintains the infrastructure after it's built?

You can choose the option that suits your team:

  • Your team maintains it. We build in your AWS accounts and repositories, then hand over the infrastructure code, documentation and runbooks, with a walkthrough so your engineers can run and change it confidently.
  • We maintain it for you. Under an ongoing support agreement, we take care of monitoring, patching, backups, security updates, cost reviews and changes, so your team can stay focused on your product.

You can also start with one option and move to the other later, for example by having us manage operations until your team is ready to take over.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We integrate AWS with the source control, CI/CD, monitoring, alerting and identity tools you already use, such as GitHub, GitLab, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Datadog, Grafana, PagerDuty and Okta. Where a tool is missing, we recommend options and explain the trade-offs.

Can you take over an environment someone else built?

Yes. We usually start with an assessment to understand what exists, bring it under code where practical, and close any urgent security gaps before taking on day-to-day operations.

Will you need access to our AWS accounts?

For most work, yes. We use role-based access you control, with multi-factor authentication, scoped permissions and logging, and you can revoke it at any time.

How do we get started?

Send us a note at support@nebulacloud.ca or use the contact form. We'll set up a short call to understand your goals, then send a written proposal with scope and pricing.

Why we exist

Many organizations move to AWS quickly and end up with environments that work but are hard to secure, expensive to run, and understood by only one or two people. Nebula Cloud was founded to fix that.

Our team brings together cloud architecture, infrastructure automation, security and operations expertise on Amazon Web Services. We build AWS environments the way we'd want to inherit them: written as code, documented, and secure by default.
